What’s Included for High School Biology?

Digitized OpenSciEd Investigations

OpenSciEd Biology investigations are designed for students to make sense of real phenomena—but managing lab data on paper can slow that process and make it harder to spot patterns across the class. Tuva digitizes investigation tables and lab worksheets so students can:

  • Enter lab data directly into the platform
  • Instantly visualize class results and compare patterns
  • Revise models and explanations as new evidence emerges
  • Spend less time organizing data and more time reasoning with evidence

This creates a stronger bridge between hands-on biology labs and the deeper scientific explanations students are expected to build.

Many OpenSciEd Biology lessons include rich datasets, but when those tables live inside PDFs or printed materials, students are limited to simply reading the data instead of truly investigating it. Tuva transforms these into fully interactive datasets so students can:

  • Sort, filter, and group information
  • Switch between graphs and representations with one click
  • Compare patterns across multiple variables and populations
  • Explore biological data the way scientists do

Students move beyond interpreting a finished graph to actively using data to test ideas, explain patterns, and support evidence-based claims.

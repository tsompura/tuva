Overview

Updated Dec 10, 2024

Learning Objectives

Students will be able to:

  • Identify the shapes of distributions (symmetric, skewed, uniform)
  • Compare the mass of different types of M&Ms using dot plots
  • Use measures of center and spread to summarize data
Driving Question
Are all M&Ms really the same size — and how would we know from the data?
Prerequisite Knowledge

Students should be familiar with reading basic dot plots and calculating mean and median. No prior Tuva experience required.

Activity Steps

4 steps · ~25 minutes total
Read 1
Explore the Dataset

Meet the data: 60 M&Ms, three types, weighed in grams.

Build 2
Make a Dot Plot

Plot mass for one type at a time. Notice the shape.

Analyze 3
Compare Distributions

Overlay plots. Which type is most consistent?

Reflect 4
Draw Conclusions

Write a claim backed by dot plot evidence.

Slide 3 · Anticipating Student Thinking

Why this activity?

  • Students often assume all candies of a "type" are identical — the data surprises them.
  • Watch for confusion between mean and median when distributions skew.
  • Listen for the word "typical" — push students to specify measure + unit.
  • Pair strategy: group by dot-plot type for the overlay step.

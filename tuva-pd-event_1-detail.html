NSTA Conference Anaheim 2026

About this Event

Join Tuva at the National Science Teaching Association Annual Conference in Anaheim, CA. Our hands-on session gives K–12 educators a deep-dive into how Tuva's data visualization tools support NGSS science and engineering practices in the classroom.

This is an in-person session. You'll leave with ready-to-use investigations, classroom facilitation strategies, and direct access to Tuva's curriculum team for follow-up support.

What You'll Learn

  • How to connect NGSS science practices to Tuva's real-world datasets
  • Strategies for guiding students from data to evidence-based claims
  • How to differentiate data tasks for diverse learners
  • Techniques for embedding Tuva investigations into existing curriculum units
  • Best practices for formative assessment using student-generated graphs

  • District curriculum coordinators
  • Anyone interested in integrating authentic data into science investigations

Grow as a Data-Literate Educator

You don't have to navigate complex datasets alone. Science starts with data, and we're here to help you make it make sense together through our expert-led sessions.

Explore Workshops View Upcoming Events

90% of educators believe data literacy is essential. Only 23% feel fully prepared to teach it.

NSTA and Tuva are closing the data confidence gap, providing the resources and pedagogical fluency needed to make complex phenomena accessible to every student.

Science Starts With Data

Educators and students making sense of the world together.

Our Approach

Our co-designed professional learning workshops build teacher capacity in three key areas:

  • Cultivating students' data skills
  • Bridging science and engineering practices with the statistical reasoning emphasized in math instruction
  • Leveraging authentic data to foster deeper sensemaking and student engagement

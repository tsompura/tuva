Bringing Authentic Data to the Center of Science Instruction

Science starts with data. Let's make it make sense together.

Sensemaking

NSTA and Tuva share a common goal: making science learning engaging, accessible, and relevant to ALL students. This goal is realized when students, as scientists, have authentic opportunities to actively make sense of the world. Our co-branded professional development is built around the four critical attributes of sensemaking: phenomena, science and engineering practices, student ideas, and science ideas. We help educators centralize these practices to transform their instruction.

Authentic Data

Data is central to the practice of science; it must also be central in the science classroom. Research shows students are more motivated when they investigate data connected to their own lives (Achieve, 2016). Building on this research, this collaboration combines NSTA's decades of leadership in science education with Tuva's expertise in data literacy and visualization. Together, we make complex, real-world data accessible for students and offer unique, classroom-ready value to educators.

Teacher Capacity

Together, NSTA and Tuva provide a cohesive system of professional learning designed to build teacher capacity in three key areas: cultivating students' data skills, bridging science and engineering practices with the statistical reasoning emphasized in math instruction, and leveraging authentic data to foster deeper sensemaking and student engagement.

About the Partners

The National Science Teaching Association (NSTA) is a vibrant community of 35,000 science educators and professionals committed to best practices in teaching science and its impact on student learning. NSTA offers high-quality science resources and continuous learning so that science educators grow professionally and excel in their career.

Visit NSTA.org

Tuva is transforming math and science education with its next-generation data, graphing, and statistical tools, used by teachers and students around the world. With over 100 million datasets, graphs, and charts uploaded and created, Tuva is the digital tool of choice for students to learn how to explore, visualize, and analyze data. More than 5 million teachers and students use the Tuva Instructional Program to bring authentic, powerful learning to the classroom.

About the Partnership

A Shared Commitment to Data-Driven Science Education

Tuva and NSTA have joined forces to provide science educators with tools, datasets, and professional learning opportunities that make authentic data analysis accessible to every classroom.

Authentic Data Analysis

Access real-world datasets from NASA, NOAA, and research institutions that bring scientific phenomena into your classroom and engage students in genuine scientific inquiry.

Teacher-Centered Professional Development

Build confidence through expert-led workshops, implementation guides, and ongoing support that helps you integrate data literacy seamlessly into your instruction.

Standards-Aligned Resources

Classroom-ready activities and lesson plans aligned to NSTA standards and science education best practices that you can use immediately with your students.

Professional Development

Professional Learning Offerings

Join our expert-led sessions to build confidence in teaching data literacy and integrating authentic data into your science instruction. All workshops include recordings, handouts, and classroom-ready materials.

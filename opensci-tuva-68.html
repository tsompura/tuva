Digitized OpenSciEd Investigations

OpenSciEd investigations are powerful—but managing student data collection on paper can slow momentum. Tuva digitizes investigation tables and worksheets so students can:

  • Enter lab data directly into the platform
  • Immediately visualize class results
  • Revise explanations as new evidence emerges
  • Spend less time organizing data and more time making sense of it

This creates a smoother bridge between hands-on labs and scientific explanation.
